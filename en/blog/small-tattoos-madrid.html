What size are small tattoos?

Most of the small tattoos I create are approximately 5–10 cm, although the ideal size depends on the design and the part of the body.

Size matters more than it might seem.

A design that looks great on a screen may need to be simplified or enlarged slightly to remain readable as a tattoo. I always take the longevity and readability of the design into consideration before tattooing it.

How to book a small tattoo in Madrid
