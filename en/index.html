About me ▾

I'm Sebastián, a queer tattoo artist based in Madrid. I create tattoos with identity, humor and references to pop culture, music, movies, TV shows and all those things we love enough to carry on our skin.

I mainly work with small and medium-sized tattoos, bold lines, color and gradients, as well as designs inspired by traditional American flash tattooing, always with my own personal touch.

My studio is located in central Madrid and is queer-friendly. I work in both English and Spanish and welcome both local clients and people visiting Madrid who want to take home a tattoo as a personal memory of their trip.

Visiting Madrid and want a tattoo?

If you're spending a few days in Madrid and want something more personal than a souvenir, you can book a tattoo with me.

I work with small tattoos, flash designs and custom pieces. I speak English and Spanish, and we can arrange your appointment around your travel dates.
